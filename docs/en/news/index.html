What happens on the pro tour, told clearly.

News · 31 Oct

Coello and Tapia win the Kuwait Major and will finish 2026 as number one

6-1, 6-1 over Arce and Tello in 55 minutes. With the title, no one can catch them in the Race. It's their fourth straight year on top. In the women's final, Triay and Brea came from behind against Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero.

News · 31 Oct

Arce and Tello will face Coello and Tapia in the Kuwait final; Ari and Andrea take on Triay and Brea

A week ago, Arce and Tello lost a final in Melilla. Today they play a Major final. Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero come through a semifinal of more than three and a half hours. Against Triay and Brea it's six wins each this year.

News · 30 Oct

Javi Leal replaces Galán at the World Cup in Qatar

The Spanish Padel Federation announced it on Friday, hours after Ale Galán pulled out. Javi Leal, 23 and world number 16, will play his first World Cup.

News · 30 Oct

Ale Galán will miss the World Cup in Qatar through injury

The Spanish Padel Federation announced it on Friday. After missing the Kuwait Major, Galán won't be in Doha either. The World Cup starts on Monday.

News · 30 Oct

Aimar Goñi will play his first Major semifinal before heading to the World Cup with Spain

With Edu Alonso, 6-1, 6-2 against Stupaczuk and Sanz, the fourth seeds. Today, in Kuwait, against Tello and Arce. Next week, Goñi plays the World Cup.

News · 29 Oct

Libaak and Alfonso beat Lebrón and Augsburger again and will face Di Nenno and Navarro

They had already beaten them in Madrid. Yesterday, in Kuwait, they did it again: in the third set, Lebrón and Augsburger won just one game. Sintes and Zamora are also into the quarterfinals. Yanguas and Nieto are out.

News · 28 Oct

Lebrón and Augsburger beat Abud and Belluati and will face Libaak and Alfonso

The qualifiers' run is over. Coello and Tapia, in under an hour, and Yanguas and Nieto, in three sets, are also into the round of 16. Today, Augsburger faces his national team partner.

News · 27 Oct

Sintes and Zamora beat Momo and Campagnolo and will play their first Major round of 16

Between them they are 38 years old. They were one game from losing and won the next three. On Wednesday, in Kuwait, they play their first round of 16 at a Major.

News · 26 Oct

Abud and Belluati come back in Kuwait and will face Lebrón and Augsburger

They came through qualifying, lost the first set and turned it around. It's Martín Abud's first main-draw win at a Major. On Tuesday, the third seeds.

News · 24 Oct

Chingotto and Galán pull out of the Kuwait Major after an injury to Ale Galán

Ale Galán is injured and the second-ranked pair won't play in Kuwait. The very place where, a year ago, they won the first FIP World Cup Pairs.

News · 22 Oct

Hexagon leaves Madrid: four venues, eight teams and goodbye to sets

The Hexagon Cup becomes the Hexagon World Series. In 2027 it starts in Madrid and ends in Abu Dhabi, with eight teams and a format with no games or sets: best of five super tie-breaks.

News · 22 Oct

These are Argentina's 16 for the World Cup in Qatar

Eight men and eight women. The men are defending the title and going for a third in a row. The women want revenge after two finals lost to Spain.

News · 21 Oct

These are Spain's 16 for the World Cup in Qatar

Eight men and eight women. From 2 to 7 November, in Doha. The men want back a title they haven't won since 2021. The women are going for a seventh in a row.

News · 20 Oct

Augsburger and Libaak launch their own brand with Siux

Two Argentinians from Siux, two new logos in one week. Leo Augsburger now signs as "Laugsburger". Tino Libaak does it with three letters: TLK.

News · 19 Oct

Coello and Tapia come back in Milan: three titles in three weeks

They dropped the first set to Augsburger and Lebrón and won 4-6, 6-3, 6-2. In the women's final, Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero beat Josemaría and González again: 7-6, 6-3.

News · 17 Oct

Augsburger and Lebrón: from 0-6 to the Milan final

They lost the first set without winning a game against Galán and Chingotto. Then they took twelve of the remaining sixteen.

News · 12 Oct

Coello and Tapia complete the double: Rotterdam and Düsseldorf

Two P2s in two weeks. Two finals against Stupaczuk and Sanz. Two titles.

News · 11 Oct

Martina Calvo and Claudia Fernández win in Düsseldorf

No contest in the final against Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero: 6-3, 6-0 in one hour and six minutes.
