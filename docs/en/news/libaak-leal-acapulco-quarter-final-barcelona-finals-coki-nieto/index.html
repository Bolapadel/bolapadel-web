News · Major de México

Libaak and Leal play for the Barcelona Finals in the Acapulco quarter-finals

Both won their round of 16 matches at the Mexico Major and tonight they meet, with a place in Barcelona at stake. Coki Nieto, 2024 Finals champion, misses out by three points.

By Luis González · 27 November 2026 · 4 min read

Tino Libaak and Javi Leal both won last night in Acapulco.

And now they are playing for the same thing. In the same match.

Tonight, in the quarter-finals of the Mexico Major, Libaak and Gonza Alfonso face Leal and Fran Guerrero. In Spain, it will already be the early hours.

On that court there is a place at the Barcelona Finals.

Two points

The Finals are played by the top 16 players in the Race.

Alfonso is practically in. Guerrero had already qualified. The ones waiting are their partners.

In Monday's Race, Leal had 3,237 points. Libaak, 3,235.

Two points.

Both have reached the quarter-finals, so they are still just as close. Tomorrow, only one of them will still be in the draw.

How they got here

Libaak and Alfonso beat Franco Stupaczuk and Jon Sanz, the third seeds: 7-6, 4-6, 6-4. More than two hours.

Libaak is 21. Since late September, with Alfonso, he hasn't gone out before the quarter-finals: Rotterdam, Düsseldorf, Milan, Kuwait, Dubai, the FIP Platinum in Aguascalientes and now Acapulco. Seven tournaments in a row. In Dubai, two weeks ago, they played the final.

Leal and Guerrero beat Edu Alonso and Aimar Goñi: 7-6, 6-7, 6-2. More than two and a half hours. The longest match of the day.

It was a revenge. A month ago, in the round of 16 at the Kuwait Major, Alonso and Goñi beat them.

Leal is 23. Three weeks ago he became world champion with Spain in Qatar.

They know each other by heart

Tonight will be the fourth time they meet in two months:

  • Round of 16 in Rotterdam (1 October): Libaak and Alfonso, 7-5, 3-6, 6-4.
  • Round of 32 in Düsseldorf (7 October): Libaak and Alfonso, 3-6, 7-6, 7-5.
  • Quarter-finals of the FIP Platinum in Aguascalientes (19 November): Leal and Guerrero, 6-4, 6-4.

Two to one for Libaak and Alfonso. But the last one, eight days ago and also in Mexico, went to Leal and Guerrero.

Coki misses out

The one who won't be in Barcelona is Coki Nieto.

Before the round of 16, he was three points away from the last place. He needed to win.

Last night, with Mike Yanguas, he lost to Íñigo Jofre and Juanlu Esbrí: 6-3, 3-6, 2-6. They started better. It wasn't enough.

According to PadelSpain, he misses out by three points.

Coki has played both Finals held so far. In 2024 he won them with Jon Sanz, beating Coello and Tapia in the final, who had won 47 matches in a row. In 2025 he played them with Yanguas.

And three weeks ago, in the World Cup final, he won his match with Coello. On the other side of the net were Libaak and Alfonso.

Yanguas will be in Barcelona. Coki, this time, won't.

The rest of the night

  • Coello and Tapia (1) beat Alex Ruiz and Javi García: 6-1, 6-7, 6-0. In the quarter-finals, Ramiro Valenzuela and Francisco Gil, who play their first ever Major quarter-final after coming back against Marc Sintes and Juan Zamora (3-6, 7-6, 6-1).
  • Lebrón and Augsburger (2) beat Sanyo Gutiérrez and Maxi Sánchez: 7-6, 6-4. Next, Jofre and Esbrí.
  • Di Nenno and Paquito Navarro (4) beat José Jiménez and Alex Chozas: 6-3, 6-4. Next, Jairo Bautista and Lucas Bergamini, who beat Momo González and Lucas Campagnolo (7-5, 6-1).

Women: Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea (1) came back against Jimena Velasco and Raquel Eugenio (3-6, 6-3, 6-3). Bea Caldera and Carmen Goenaga beat Alejandra Salazar and Aranzazu Osoro (6-3, 6-4). And Marina Guinart and Alejandra Alonso, after their 6-1, 6-3 win over Jessica Castelló and Nuria Rodríguez, have secured their place in Barcelona, according to Premier Padel.

Tonight

Libaak, 21. Leal, 23.

Two points in the Race.

One match.

And Barcelona, on the other side of the net.

— Luis González
