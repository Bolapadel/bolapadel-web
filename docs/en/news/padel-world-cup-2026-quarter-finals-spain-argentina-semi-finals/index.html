Spain and Argentina put both their teams into the World Cup semi-finals in Qatar

Spain beat Brazil 2-0, with Coello and Javi Leal on the same side, and Argentina beat Germany 3-0. The women, two more 3-0s. They can only meet in Saturday's finals.

By Luis González · 5 November 2026 · 4 min read

Arturo Coello: six days, four partners.

On Saturday he won Kuwait with Tapia. At the World Cup, with Paquito Navarro on Monday and with Aimar Goñi on Tuesday. On Wednesday he didn't play.

And today, Javi Leal.

The same Javi Leal who, this summer in Pretoria, beat Coello and Tapia in a semi-final.

Today they were on the same side of the net. And Spain are into the semi-finals.

Spain: two points were enough

Spain beat Brazil 2-0. The first two matches decided the tie and the third wasn't played.

  • Coki Nieto and Jon Sanz: 6-2, 6-2 over Fernandes and De Avila Simonato.
  • Javi Leal and Coello: 6-2, 6-1 over Freitas and Correa.

Lebrón and Goñi, down for the third match, didn't get on court.

Nieto and Sanz, who hadn't played together at this World Cup, opened the tie.

Javi Leal joined the squad on Friday, after Ale Galán's injury. He has played three matches, each with a different partner: Yanguas on Tuesday, Lebrón on Wednesday and Coello today.

Three matches. Three wins.

Wednesday has a story to it. Lebrón was the partner Galán was going to play with again in Doha.

Spain have played eleven matches at the World Cup. They have won them all.

Tomorrow, a semi-final against Portugal or the Netherlands, who were playing for their place as this was written.

Argentina: a day off for Tapia

Argentina beat Germany 3-0. They dropped nine games.

  • Chingotto and Alfonso: 6-1, 6-1 over Reca and Wunner.
  • Augsburger and Di Nenno: 6-0, 6-4 over Lindmeyer and Fitzon.
  • Stupaczuk and Arce: 6-3, 6-0 over Boehnke and Merten.

Tapia and Libaak didn't play. They did on Wednesday, against France, and it was hard work: they lost the first set and came back to win 5-7, 6-3, 6-4.

Argentina, champions at the last two World Cups, have played twelve matches and won twelve.

Tomorrow, Italy or Sweden.

They can only meet in the final

Spain and Argentina are in opposite halves of the draw. If both win tomorrow, they play for the World Cup on Saturday.

Just like 2024. Argentina won that final, 2-1.

The women: not a set dropped

Spain beat Sweden 3-0.

  • Paula Josemaría and Bea González: 6-0, 6-1 over Behram and Sjöland.
  • Marta Ortega and Martina Calvo: 6-0, 6-2 over Andersson and Girdo.
  • Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero: 6-1, 6-2 over Kjaergaard and Björk.

Twelve matches at the World Cup. Twelve wins. Not a single set dropped. Fifteen games conceded in total.

They are going for a seventh World Cup in a row. Tomorrow, France.

Argentina beat Portugal 3-0. Delfi Brea and Claudia Jensen, 6-0, 6-0. Tomorrow, Italy.

The women's teams, too, can only meet in the final.

Tomorrow, the semi-finals

  • 12:00: the women. Spain v France and Argentina v Italy.
  • In the afternoon: the men. Spain v Portugal or the Netherlands and Argentina v Italy or Sweden.

Times are CET; in Argentina, four hours earlier. Where to watch, here.

Last Saturday, Coello and Tapia lifted the Kuwait trophy together.

This Saturday they could be in the same final.

Each on his own bench.

— Luis González
