Ale Galán will miss the World Cup in Qatar through injury

The Spanish Padel Federation announced it on Friday. After missing the Kuwait Major, Galán won't be in Doha either. The World Cup starts on Monday.

By Luis González · 30 October 2026 · 2 min read

It had been feared for a week. Today it's official.

Ale Galán won't play the World Cup in Qatar.

The Spanish Padel Federation announced it on Friday morning. Medical leave. No further details.

We don't know what the injury is. Or how long he'll be out.

The Federation wishes him a speedy recovery. So do we.

First Kuwait, now Doha

Last Saturday he pulled out of the Kuwait Major. He announced it himself on Instagram.

From that day on, the question was a different one. Would he make it to Doha?

He won't.

His last match was on 17 October, in the Milan semi-finals. He and Chingotto won the first set 6-0 against Lebrón and Augsburger. They lost the next two: 6-2, 6-2.

No reunion

That night, on the other side of the net, was Juan Lebrón.

With him, Galán was world number 1. And with him he was going to play again in Doha: in early October, Spain coach Juanjo Gutiérrez said they would share a court again at the World Cup.

There will be no reunion.

A gap on the backhand side

Galán is the world number 3 and plays on the backhand side.

Without him, Spain have seven of the eight players called up.

The Federation hasn't said whether it will call up another player. According to Padel Addict, the deadline is tomorrow, Saturday.

When the squad was picked, the last spot went to Aimar Goñi, ahead of Momo González and Javi Leal. Both play on the backhand side.

Goñi, by the way, plays the Kuwait Major semi-final today. Along the way he beat Javi Leal.

Monday, Doha

The World Cup runs from 2 to 7 November.

Spain haven't won the men's title since 2021. Argentina took the last two.

Chingotto, his partner on tour, is in Argentina's squad.

Six days ago he wrote that he hoped to be back on court soon.

It will have to be after Doha.

— Luis González
