News · Major de Kuwait

Coello and Tapia win the Kuwait Major and will finish 2026 as number one

6-1, 6-1 over Arce and Tello in 55 minutes. With the title, no one can catch them in the Race. It's their fourth straight year on top. In the women's final, Triay and Brea came from behind against Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero.

By Luis González · 31 October 2026 · 3 min read

6-1, 6-1. Fifty-five minutes.

Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia are the Kuwait Major champions.

And the title brings something else with it. They will finish 2026 as number one.

It's the fourth year in a row.

The numbers

They arrived in Kuwait 2,840 points ahead of Ale Galán and Fede Chingotto in the Race.

The title is worth 2,000. The lead goes up to 4,840.

Three tournaments are left: Dubai, Mexico and the Finals in Barcelona. Even if Galán and Chingotto won all three, they would add 4,500.

Not enough. Those are Padel Addict's numbers.

Galán and Chingotto couldn't play in Kuwait. Galán's injury kept them out. It has also ruled him out of the World Cup.

Four years on top

2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026.

They teamed up in February 2023. They won their very first tournament together, in Abu Dhabi.

That year they finished number one on the World Padel Tour. Since 2024, in the FIP ranking.

Without dropping serve

The final was one-way traffic.

Coello and Tapia didn't drop a single service game. They won 62% of the points.

This year they had won finals 6-2, 6-2. None as clear-cut as this one.

For Maxi Arce, it was his first Major final. Juan Tello had already played one against them: Málaga, 2023, with Álex Ruiz. He lost that one too.

A week ago, Arce and Tello lost the FIP Gold final in Melilla. Today, a Major final. They leave Kuwait with their best tournament together.

Thirteen titles

Coello and Tapia have won 13 titles this year. They've played 18 finals.

The last one they lost was in May, in Buenos Aires. Since then, eleven finals. Eleven titles.

The women: Triay and Brea

Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea won the women's final 3-6, 6-1, 6-1 against Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero. One hour and 56 minutes.

Ari and Andrea took the first set. After that, just two games.

They had met twelve times this year. It was six all, with the last three going to Ari and Andrea. Now it's seven to six for the number ones.

It's Triay and Brea's seventh title of the year. And in the Race they move 2,780 points clear of Ari and Andrea, according to Premier Padel's numbers.

Ari and Andrea close out their fifth final in a row. They've won three.

And on Monday, the World Cup

Next stop, Doha. The World Cup starts on Monday.

Coello, with Spain. Tapia, with Argentina. Today they lifted the trophy together. Next week, on opposite sides.

Same for Triay and Brea.

— Luis González
