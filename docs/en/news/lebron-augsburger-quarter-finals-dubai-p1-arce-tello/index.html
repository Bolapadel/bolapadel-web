News · Dubái P1

Lebrón and Augsburger beat Víctor Ruiz and Rodrigo and will face Arce and Tello

With Coello and Tapia and Galán and Chingotto missing, they're the highest-ranked pair at the Dubai P1. On Friday, in the quarter-finals, the Kuwait finalists.

By Luis González · 12 November 2026 · 4 min read

Lebrón and Augsburger are into the Dubai P1 quarter-finals.

Today they beat Víctor Ruiz and Facundo Rodrigo 6-2, 6-4. In an hour and nine minutes.

Wednesday was harder. They lost the first set to Castaño and Pol Hernández and came back: 4-6, 6-3, 6-3.

The highest-ranked pair

Coello and Tapia aren't in Dubai, because of Tapia's injury. Neither are Galán and Chingotto.

Without the top two, Lebrón and Augsburger are the highest-ranked pair in the draw. Second seeds.

This year they've played four Premier finals. All four against Coello and Tapia. They won one: Brussels, in April.

In Dubai, Coello and Tapia won't be on the other side of the net.

From the World Cup to Dubai

On Saturday, in the World Cup final, they were on opposite sides of the net. Lebrón and Goñi beat Augsburger and Di Nenno: 5-7, 7-5, 6-2.

Each in his own shirt.

Five days later, together again. And winning.

Arce and Tello, again

On Friday, in the quarter-finals: Maxi Arce and Juan Tello (8). Finalists in Kuwait two weeks ago.

Today they came back against Goñi and Edu Alonso (9): 3-6, 6-3, 6-2. They had already beaten them in the Kuwait semi-finals.

Lebrón and Augsburger against Arce and Tello has been played three times this year: London, Rotterdam and Düsseldorf.

Lebrón and Augsburger won all three.

The rest of the day

Stupaczuk and Sanz (3) beat Sanyo and Maxi Sánchez (15) 6-3, 6-4.

In the quarter-finals they'll face Juani De Pascual and Javi Garrido (14), who beat Yanguas and Nieto (5) through two tie-breaks: 7-6, 3-6, 7-6. Two hours and 34 minutes, the longest match of the day.

Bautista and Bergamini (11) came back against Guerrero and Leal (6): 1-6, 7-5, 6-3.

They'll play Libaak and Alfonso (10), who beat the lucky loser pair, Marcos González and Diego García, 6-2, 6-3. It's their fifth Premier quarter-final in a row: Rotterdam, Düsseldorf, Milan, Kuwait and Dubai.

Momo and Campagnolo (7) beat Jofre and Esbrí (12) 6-3, 6-3 in under an hour. Their opponents: Flavio Abbate and Franco Dal Bianco, who beat Roglan and Aragón 7-6, 6-3.

That ends the run of Roglan and Aragón, who came back against Paquito and Di Nenno yesterday. Five matches in five days, starting in qualifying.

One more thing: Spain won the World Cup with eight players. Two of them are still in the Dubai quarter-finals: Lebrón and Sanz.

Women

The top four pairs are through to the quarter-finals.

  • Triay and Brea (1): 6-3, 6-1 against Talaván and Saiz.
  • Josemaría and Bea González (2): 6-3, 6-1 against Fassio and Virseda.
  • Ari and Andrea (3): 6-0, 6-4 against Manquillo and Lucía Sainz, in an hour.
  • Calvo and Claudia Fernández (4): 7-6, 6-4 against Castelló and Rodríguez.

The surprise came from Jimena Velasco and Raquel Eugenio. They came back against Araújo and Ortega (5): 4-6, 6-4, 6-3, in two hours and eight minutes. Eugenio is 18. On Friday, Ari and Andrea.

The quarter-finals

Men:

  • Libaak / Alfonso (10) – Bautista / Bergamini (11)
  • De Pascual / Garrido (14) – Stupaczuk / Sanz (3)
  • Abbate / Dal Bianco – Momo / Campagnolo (7)
  • Arce / Tello (8) – Lebrón / Augsburger (2)

Women:

  • Triay / Brea (1) – Jensen / Icardo (6)
  • Rufo / Iglesias – Calvo / Fernández (4)
  • Ari / Andrea (3) – Velasco / Eugenio
  • Guinart / Alonso (7) – Josemaría / Bea González (2)

On Saturday, Lebrón and Augsburger were playing for a World Cup against each other.

On Friday, together, they play for a place in the Dubai semi-finals.

— Luis González
