News · Major de Kuwait

Arce and Tello will face Coello and Tapia in the Kuwait final; Ari and Andrea take on Triay and Brea

A week ago, Arce and Tello lost a final in Melilla. Today they play a Major final. Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero come through a semifinal of more than three and a half hours. Against Triay and Brea it's six wins each this year.

By Luis González · 31 October 2026 · 3 min read

A week ago, Maxi Arce and Juan Tello lost a final in Melilla.

A FIP Gold. 6-1, 6-4 to Lucas Bergamini and Jairo Bautista.

Today they play another one. The Kuwait Major final.

Across the net: Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia.

6-4, 6-3 over Goñi and Alonso

Arce and Tello, the ninth seeds, beat Aimar Goñi and Edu Alonso 6-4, 6-3. One hour and 19 minutes.

That ends Goñi's first Major semifinal. Next stop, Doha: the World Cup starts on Monday and he's going with Spain.

The first one

Arce and Tello had never gone past the quarterfinals together in Premier Padel. Now, a final.

For Arce, from Salta, left-handed, 28, it's his first Major final. Tello, from Córdoba, 31, hadn't played a Major final since Paris 2022, according to Premier Padel.

And Tello knows what a final against Coello and Tapia feels like. Málaga, July 2023. With Álex Ruiz. They lost 7-5, 7-6.

Coello and Tapia, in under an hour

Coello and Tapia beat Tino Libaak and Gonzalo Alfonso 6-2, 6-2.

Libaak and Alfonso had just beaten Lebrón and Augsburger again. Against the number ones, four games.

Coello and Tapia have won 12 titles this year. They've won their last ten finals.

They have only played Arce and Tello once. Round of 16 in Madrid, in September: 7-6, 6-3.

If they win, they lock up number one in the Race, according to Premier Padel.

Ari and Andrea, to the limit again

Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero beat Paula Josemaría and Bea González, the second seeds, 4-6, 7-6, 7-6.

Two tie-breaks. More than three and a half hours. In the end, each pair won half the points.

It's not the first time. In June, in the Italy Major semifinal, it went past four hours. Ari and Andrea won that one too.

The next day, in the final, Triay and Brea were waiting. They lost 6-1, 7-5.

Today, the same. Triay and Brea.

Andrea is 19. Ari, 29. It's their fifth final in a row: Paris, Rotterdam, Düsseldorf, Milan and Kuwait. They've won three.

Triay and Brea, also in three sets

Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea, the number ones, beat Claudia Fernández and Martina Calvo 6-4, 2-6, 7-6. More than three hours.

Six all

It will be the thirteenth time they meet this year. Six wins each.

The last three went to Ari and Andrea: Rotterdam, Düsseldorf and Milan.

If Ari and Andrea win, they'll be 1,180 points behind Triay and Brea in the Race. If Triay and Brea win, the gap grows to 2,780. Those are Premier Padel's numbers. Dubai, Mexico and the Finals are left.

And on Monday, the World Cup

Coello and Tapia, partners today. From Monday, in Doha, each with his own national team. Same for Triay and Brea.

Arce is also going to the World Cup with Argentina. Ari and Andrea, with Spain.

Schedule (Spanish time, CET)

  • 14:00 · Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero vs Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea.
  • Not before 17:00 · Maxi Arce and Juan Tello vs Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia.

— Luis González
