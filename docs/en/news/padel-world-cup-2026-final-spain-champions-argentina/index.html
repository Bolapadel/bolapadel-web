Spain beat Argentina in both World Cup finals in Qatar

The men won 2-1 and ended Argentina's run as 2022 and 2024 champions. Coello and Nieto sealed the final. The women won 2-0: a seventh straight World Cup.

By Luis González · 7 November 2026 · 5 min read

Eight days. Five partners. Two titles.

Last Saturday, Arturo Coello won the Kuwait Major with Tapia. At the World Cup he played with Paquito, with Goñi, with Javi Leal. And today, with Coki Nieto, he closed out the final.

6-3, 6-2 over Libaak and Alfonso. The third match. The decider.

Spain are world champions. 2-1 over Argentina, who were chasing a third World Cup in a row.

And the women, 2-0. A seventh in a row.

The final, match by match

  • Juan Lebrón and Aimar Goñi: 5-7, 7-5, 6-2 over Di Nenno and Augsburger, in an hour and 59 minutes.
  • Paquito Navarro and Jon Sanz: lost 6-1, 6-2 to Tapia and Chingotto.
  • Arturo Coello and Coki Nieto: 6-3, 6-2 over Libaak and Alfonso, in an hour and 19 minutes.

Lebrón against his partner

Lebrón and Augsburger are partners on tour. Today, they were on opposite sides of the net.

Argentina took the first set. Lebrón and Goñi turned it around.

Goñi's third comeback of the World Cup. On Monday, against Sweden. On Friday, against Portugal. Today, in the final. All three after losing the first set.

Eight days ago he played his first Major semi-final. Today he won the first point of a World Cup final.

Tapia won his match. It wasn't enough

Tapia and Chingotto won 6-1, 6-2 in 54 minutes. It was Spain's first defeat of the whole World Cup.

1-1. Everything down to the third match.

Coello and Nieto: 2024 again, but with the prize

In the final two years ago, Coello and Nieto also won their match: 6-1, 6-2. It wasn't enough. Argentina took the other two.

Today they had the last one. And this time, it was enough.

A fifth men's World Cup for Spain. The first since 2021, also in Doha.

Two years ago, in this same city, Argentina won 2-1. Today, 2-1 to Spain.

Juanjo Gutiérrez, the head coach, was already on the bench in 2021. This time he arrived in Doha without Galán, ruled out injured three days before the World Cup. And he kept changing the pairs day by day.

The women: a seventh straight World Cup

  • Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero: 6-3, 6-3 over Osoro and Fassio.
  • Paula Josemaría and Gemma Triay: 6-2, 6-7, 6-3 over Delfi Brea and Claudia Jensen, in two hours and 23 minutes.

Bea González and Claudia Fernández didn't need to play.

The set Josemaría and Triay dropped was the first set Spain lost in the whole World Cup.

Triay beat Brea, her partner on tour. A week ago they won Kuwait together.

A tenth World Cup for Spain. A seventh in a row. Carolina Navarro's first as head coach. Their final, here.

Each in their own shirt

Last Saturday, in Kuwait, Coello and Tapia lifted the same trophy. So did Triay and Brea.

This Saturday they played for the World Cup. Each in their own shirt.

The gold stays in Spain.

Both times.

— Luis González
