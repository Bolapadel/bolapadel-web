Spain and Argentina start the World Cup in Qatar with a 3-0

Argentina dropped three games in three matches against Australia. Spain beat Sweden, with a scare in Yanguas and Goñi's match. The women also started with clean sweeps. Today, Paraguay and the Netherlands.

By Luis González · 3 November 2026 · 4 min read

On Saturday, Coello and Tapia lifted the Kuwait trophy together.

On Monday, Coello played with Paquito Navarro. Tapia didn't play.

Day one of the World Cup in Qatar. Argentina and Spain both started with a 3-0. One without breaking a sweat. The other, with a scare.

Argentina: three games in three matches

The champions made their debut on court 5, with the stands full. Against Australia, back at a World Cup eight years later.

  • Stupaczuk and Chingotto: 6-1, 6-1.
  • Augsburger and Di Nenno: 6-0, 6-0. In half an hour.
  • Arce and Alfonso: 6-1, 6-0.

Six sets. Three games dropped. Across the three matches, one hour and 46 minutes on court.

Keep an eye on the last pair. Arce and Alfonso are Argentina's two World Cup debutants at senior level. And Arce had played the Kuwait final on Saturday, losing to Coello and Tapia. Two days later, 6-1 and 6-0.

Tapia and Libaak, the man who won the point that decided the 2024 World Cup, didn't play.

Spain: the scare, with Goñi

Spain also won 3-0. Against Sweden. But not as cleanly.

  • Coello and Paquito Navarro: 6-3, 6-2 over Vásquez and Windahl.
  • Yanguas and Goñi: 6-7, 7-5, 6-2 over Axelsson and Slaryd.
  • Lebrón and Jon Sanz: 6-0, 6-2 over Olsson and Frost.

Yanguas and Goñi's match was the one that sealed the tie for Spain. And it got complicated. They lost the first set on a tie-break, 12-10. They took the second 7-5. Two hours and 13 minutes.

Goñi is the newcomer in the squad. On Friday he played his first Major semi-final in Kuwait. He lost it to Arce and Tello. On Monday, his first match at a World Cup.

He started out suffering. But winning.

Javi Leal, who came in for the injured Ale Galán, and Coki Nieto didn't play.

The closest ties, away from the favourites

The Netherlands beat France 2-1, in Argentina's group. It came down to the third match: Van Opstal and Prins beat Leygue and Blanqué 6-4, 1-6, 6-3.

Germany also beat Chile 2-1 in the third match. And from behind: they lost the first set of the deciding match and won the next two.

The rest, no surprises. Paraguay, Portugal, Italy and Brazil beat the UAE, Qatar, the United States and Mexico 3-0.

The women, ruthless

Spain beat Germany 3-0. They dropped two games in the whole tie.

Gemma Triay, two days after becoming the player with the most titles in history, won 6-0, 6-0 with Marta Ortega. Claudia Fernández and Bea González, 6-0, 6-1. Paula Josemaría and Martina Calvo, 6-1, 6-0.

Argentina beat Chile 3-0. Riera and Bidahorria, 6-0, 6-1.

Today

  • 12:00: Spain v Great Britain and Argentina v Belgium, in the women's draw.
  • From 16:00: Spain v Paraguay and Argentina v the Netherlands, in the men's.

Paraguay also won all three matches. Their team includes Martín Abud, whom Lebrón and Augsburger beat a week ago in Kuwait.

Argentina face the Netherlands, the team that beat France.

Times are CET. Where to watch, here.

Coello has already won with another partner.

Tapia hasn't had his turn yet.

— Luis González
