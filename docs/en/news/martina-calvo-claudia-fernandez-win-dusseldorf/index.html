News · Germany P2

Martina Calvo and Claudia Fernández win in Düsseldorf

No contest in the final against Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero: 6-3, 6-0 in one hour and six minutes.

By Luis González · 11 October 2026 · 1 min read

They came into Germany P2 as fourth seeds. They left with the title.

Martina Calvo and Claudia Fernández beat third seeds Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero in the final. And they left no gaps: 6-3, 6-0.

A bagel in the second set.

Some context: Sánchez and Ustero had won Rotterdam the week before. Here they couldn't find a way in.

— Luis González
