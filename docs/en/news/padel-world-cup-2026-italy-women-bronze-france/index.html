Italy beat France for a fourth straight bronze at the women's World Cup

Italy's women beat France 2-0 in the third-place match. Bronze in 2021, in 2022, in 2024 and now again in Doha.

By Luis González · 7 November 2026 · 2 min read

Fourth World Cup in a row. Fourth bronze in a row.

Italy beat France 2-0 in the third-place match. Once again, right behind Spain and Argentina.

In the three previous editions, Italy's bronze was decided in the third match. This time it wasn't needed.

Baldi and Sussarello: from defeat to comeback

Yesterday, in the semi-final against Argentina, Caterina Baldi and Giulia Sussarello lost 6-2, 6-2 to Osoro and Fassio.

Today they started by losing again. 4-6 against Pothier and Touly.

And they turned it around: 6-1, 6-3. Two hours and five minutes on court.

"For the first time I really felt the weight of responsibility," said Baldi.

Dal Pozzo and Stellato: aged 21 and 44

Yesterday, Giulia Dal Pozzo played with Giorgia Marchetti. Today, captain Marcela Ferrari paired her with Emily Stellato.

One is 21. The other, 44.

They beat Collombon and Bahurel 7-6, 6-3, in an hour and 36 minutes.

"Every ball weighs a lot," said Stellato. Today, each one weighed a bronze medal.

Orsi and Marchetti, down for the third match, didn't need to play it.

Four bronzes, three in Doha

  • 2021, Doha: against France.
  • 2022, Dubai: 2-1 over Belgium.
  • 2024, Doha: against Portugal.
  • 2026, Doha: 2-0 over France.

France, beaten 2-0 by Spain yesterday, finish fourth. Just like five years ago, also in Doha.

Ferrari sums it up in one sentence: "Hard work pays off."

Ahead of them, Spain and Argentina. The usual two.

Right behind, Italy. Also as usual.

— Luis González
