News · Major de Kuwait

Libaak and Alfonso beat Lebrón and Augsburger again and will face Di Nenno and Navarro

They had already beaten them in Madrid. Yesterday, in Kuwait, they did it again: in the third set, Lebrón and Augsburger won just one game. Sintes and Zamora are also into the quarterfinals. Yanguas and Nieto are out.

By Luis González · 29 October 2026 · 4 min read

Yesterday morning I told you that Leo Augsburger was playing against his national team partner.

Juan Lebrón and Augsburger against Tino Libaak and Gonzalo Alfonso. Round of 16 at the Kuwait Major.

In September, in Madrid, Libaak and Alfonso won.

Yesterday it happened again. 4-6, 7-5, 6-1. Two hours and eight minutes.

A 6-1 to finish

Lebrón and Augsburger, the third seeds, took the first set.

The second went to 7-5. For Libaak and Alfonso.

And the third was theirs from start to finish. They won 65% of the points. They didn't drop serve once.

6-1.

Two matches against Lebrón and Augsburger. Two wins. Both in the round of 16.

Today, Di Nenno and Navarro

Libaak and Alfonso, the 12th seeds, reach the quarterfinals for the fourth tournament in a row: Rotterdam, Düsseldorf, Milan and now Kuwait.

In the previous three, that's as far as they went. Twice they lost to Coello and Tapia. The other time, to Galán and Chingotto.

Today they face Martín Di Nenno and Paquito Navarro, the fifth seeds. Not before 16:30 Spanish time.

And next week, Libaak and Augsburger will be on the same side again: both are going with Argentina to the World Cup in Doha. So is Alfonso.

Sintes and Zamora keep going

On Tuesday I told you that Marc Sintes and Juan Zamora, «Coquito», were about to play their first round of 16 at a Major.

They won it. 6-3, 7-5 against Javier García and Álex Ruiz, a seeded pair. One hour and 29 minutes.

Sintes is 20. Zamora, 18. They are the youngest pair in the quarterfinals.

And the lowest-ranked: Sintes is world number 46; Zamora, number 83.

Today they open the day at 12:00 Spanish time. Across the net: Juan Tello and Maxi Arce, the ninth seeds.

Yanguas and Nieto, out

Jairo Bautista and Lucas Bergamini lost the first set 6-1.

And won the next two: 6-4, 6-2.

Out go Mike Yanguas and Coki Nieto, the sixth seeds. It was Nieto's return after missing Düsseldorf and Milan.

Bautista and Bergamini have played together since the start of October. Today, against Coello and Tapia. Not before 18:00.

Coello and Tapia, in 48 minutes

The world number ones beat Enrique Goenaga and Guillermo Collado: 6-2, 6-1.

They didn't drop serve once.

The rest

  • Franco Stupaczuk and Jon Sanz, the fourth seeds, beat Iñigo Jofre and Juanlu Esbrí: 6-2, 7-5.
  • Aimar Goñi and Edu Alonso knocked out Javi Leal and Fran Guerrero, the seventh seeds: 6-4, 6-4. Today, against Stupaczuk and Sanz.
  • Di Nenno and Navarro beat Pol Hernández and Manu Castaño: 6-3, 6-0.
  • Tello and Arce beat Denis Perino and Facundo Domínguez in three sets: 6-3, 3-6, 6-1.

In the women's draw

Julieta Bidahorria and Virginia Riera's run is over: Lorena Rufo and Victoria Iglesias beat them 6-2, 6-2.

Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea (6-0, 6-1) and Bea González and Paula Josemaría (6-1, 6-0) gave up just one game each. Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero, the Milan champions, won 6-2, 6-1.

The upset came from Giulia Dal Pozzo and Nuria Rodríguez: almost three hours to beat Marina Guinart and Alejandra Alonso, the seventh seeds (6-7, 6-3, 6-2).

Today, the quarterfinals

Sintes and Zamora, at midday.

Libaak and Alfonso, in the afternoon.

Coello and Tapia, to close the day.

Augsburger is no longer in Kuwait. But next week, in Doha, Libaak will have him by his side again.

— Luis González
