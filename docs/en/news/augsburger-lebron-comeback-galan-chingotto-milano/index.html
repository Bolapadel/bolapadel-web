Augsburger and Lebrón: from 0-6 to the Milan final

They lost the first set without winning a game against Galán and Chingotto. Then they took twelve of the remaining sixteen.

By Luis González · 17 October 2026 · 1 min read

A 0-6 set in a P1 semifinal usually tells you how it ends.

Not this time.

Third seeds Leo Augsburger and Juan Lebrón didn't win a single game in the first set. On the other side: Alejandro Galán and Federico Chingotto, second in the draw and second in the Race.

What came next was a different match. 6-2. Then 6-2 again.

One hour and twenty-three minutes to turn it all around.

On Sunday they play the final against Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia.

— Luis González
