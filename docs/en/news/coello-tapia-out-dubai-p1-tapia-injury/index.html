Coello and Tapia pull out of the Dubai P1 after an injury to Tapia

Agustín Tapia is injured and the world number ones won't play in Dubai, where they were going for a third straight title. A lucky loser pair will take their place in the draw.

By Luis González · 10 November 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026 · 3 min read

Update, 11 November. The lucky loser pair are Marcos González and Diego García. On Wednesday they beat Chozas and Rubini and on Thursday they play Libaak and Alfonso in the last 16.

Coello and Tapia won't play the Dubai P1.

Agustín Tapia is injured. The tournament announced it tonight, along with the updated men's main draw.

Nobody has said what the injury is. Or how long he'll be out.

Their place will go to a lucky loser pair. One of the pairs that lost in qualifying and now gets a second chance.

Get well soon, Agustín.

Going for three in a row

Dubai is their tournament. They won it in 2024 and in 2025.

Both times in the final against Galán and Chingotto. And both times with an almost identical score: 6-4, 6-3 the first year, 6-3, 6-4 the second.

Galán and Chingotto aren't there this year either, because of Ale Galán's injury.

So on Sunday, the Dubai P1 will have new champions.

Number one is already theirs

Dubai doesn't touch what matters. Coello and Tapia already sealed the year-end number one in Kuwait, ten days ago.

Then, each in his own shirt, they played the World Cup final in Qatar. Tapia won his match, with Chingotto. Coello won his, with Coki Nieto.

Dubai was going to be the reunion. It will have to wait.

Who finds the gap

Chozas and Rubini won their first match on Tuesday: 6-2, 6-4 against Fernández and Cepero. On Wednesday they were due to face the world number ones. Now they'll play the lucky loser pair.

In that quarter of the draw, the top seeds are now Guerrero and Leal (6). Javi Leal, a world champion with Spain on Saturday. And the pair who beat Coello and Tapia in the Pretoria semi-finals this summer: 7-5, 7-5.

In the whole top half, the favourites are now Stupaczuk and Sanz (3). In the bottom half wait Lebrón and Augsburger (2), now the highest-ranked pair in the tournament.

What's left of the year

After Dubai, two events remain: the Mexico Major, from 23 to 29 November, and the Finals in Barcelona, from 10 to 13 December.

Coello and Tapia will be back. This week, Dubai belongs to someone else.

— Luis González
