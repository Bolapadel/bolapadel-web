Lebrón and Augsburger win the Dubai P1 against Libaak and Alfonso

Third time lucky. The pair that had beaten them both previous times met them in the final, and this time the title went to Lebrón and Augsburger. In the women's draw, Martina Calvo and Claudia Fernández are the champions.

By Luis González · 15 November 2026 · 4 min read

Third time lucky.

Libaak and Alfonso had beaten them twice this year. In Madrid and in Kuwait. Both times in the round of 16.

Today they met in a final. And this time it went their way.

Juan Lebrón and Leo Augsburger (2) are the Dubai P1 champions. They beat Tino Libaak and Gonzalo Alfonso (10) 7-6, 5-7, 6-3. Almost two and a half hours of padel.

Two titles, two walls

Five finals in 2026.

The first four, against Coello and Tapia. They won one, in Brussels, in April.

The fifth was the first without the world number ones on the other side. And they won it.

Two titles together. One, against the number ones. The other, against the pair that always beat them.

One break in the third

The first set went to a tie-break. In Madrid they lost it that way. Today they won it.

The second, 7-5 to Libaak and Alfonso.

In the third, Lebrón and Augsburger didn't drop serve. One break was enough.

In the end, they won 52% of the points. That's how close it was.

Lebrón admitted afterwards that they switched off a little in the second set. And he explained what holds them together: "Whatever happens, we have to stay together at all times."

Eight days after the World Cup

On Saturday 7, in Doha, Lebrón beat Augsburger in the World Cup final. Each in his own shirt.

Augsburger was coming off that defeat. Today he thanked his team for their support these past days and asked for something simple: to enjoy it and switch off.

Eight days later, they lift a title together.

Libaak and Alfonso, one step away

Libaak and Alfonso also lost that World Cup final. Today they miss out on their first title together.

But they leave Dubai with Libaak's first Premier Padel final, at 21. And with a run that hasn't stopped since October: quarter-finals in Rotterdam, Düsseldorf and Milan, semi-finals in Kuwait and a final in Dubai.

Women: Claudia Fernández wins Dubai again

Martina Calvo and Claudia Fernández (4) are the Dubai P1 champions. They beat Paula Josemaría and Bea González (2) 7-5, 6-1.

They had met six times this year, always in the semi-finals. Three wins each. The first final went to Martina and Claudia.

It's their third title together in 2026, after London and Düsseldorf. And it comes eight days after winning the World Cup with Spain.

Claudia Fernández already won Dubai last year. With Bea González.

Nine days ago, in the World Cup semi-final, they played together again for Spain. Today, each on one side of the net.

According to Premier Padel, Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea only need to reach the semi-finals in Mexico to secure the number one spot.

What's next

The Mexico Major is left, in Acapulco, from 23 to 29 November. That's where the last places for the Barcelona Finals, from 10 to 13 December, will be decided.

The Dubai P1 is in its third edition. Coello and Tapia won the first two.

Since Tuesday we knew this year would bring new champions.

Now it has them. And they're celebrating together.

— Luis González
