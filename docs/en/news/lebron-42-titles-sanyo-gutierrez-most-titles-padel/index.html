Lebrón reaches 42 titles and draws level with Sanyo Gutiérrez

With the Dubai P1, Juan Lebrón reaches 42 titles and draws level with Sanyo Gutiérrez among the active players with the most trophies. Only Tapia, Galán and Coello have more.

By Luis González · 20 November 2026 · 2 min read

42.

On Sunday, Juan Lebrón won the Dubai P1 with Leo Augsburger.

That title came with something extra. With it, Lebrón reaches 42 and draws level with Sanyo Gutiérrez among the active players with the most titles.

That is Padel Addict's count, which adds up Premier Padel, World Padel Tour and Padel Pro Tour.

Only three ahead

This is the list of active players:

  • Agustín Tapia: 66
  • Ale Galán: 60
  • Arturo Coello: 59
  • Juan Lebrón and Sanyo Gutiérrez: 42
  • Paquito Navarro: 26
  • Fede Chingotto and Maxi Sánchez: 22
  • Franco Stupaczuk: 19
  • Martín Di Nenno: 15

Tapia, at 27, is already level with Pablo Lima on the all-time list. Only Fernando Belasteguín (162) and Juan Martín Díaz (116) have more.

Coello, at 24, is one behind Galán.

None of the three played in Dubai.

Most of them, with Galán

Most of those 42 titles came alongside Ale Galán. The last one they won together was Riyadh, in March 2024.

Since then, Lebrón has added four more, with three different partners:

  • Martín Di Nenno: Finland, 2024.
  • Franco Stupaczuk: Cancún, 2025.
  • Leo Augsburger: Brussels and Dubai, 2026.

Two tournaments to go clear

There are two tournaments left in 2026: the Mexico Major, from 23 to 29 November, and the Finals in Barcelona, from 10 to 13 December.

Two chances to take fourth place on his own.

Women: Triay still on top

Gemma Triay leads the women's list, with 59. Behind her, Alejandra Salazar and Ari Sánchez, with 58, and Paula Josemaría, with 52.

The biggest mover this week is Claudia Fernández. With the Dubai title alongside Martina Calvo, she reaches 15, one behind Marta Ortega. She is 20.

Sanyo: 42 titles and 42 years old.

Lebrón: the same titles. And eleven years younger.

— Luis González
