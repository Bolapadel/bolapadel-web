Spain and Argentina will meet in both World Cup finals in Qatar

Spain beat Portugal 2-0 and the women beat France 2-0. Argentina beat Italy 2-0 in both draws. On Saturday, a double final in Doha, just like 2021 and 2024.

By Luis González · 6 November 2026 · 3 min read

Coello didn't play today.

Tapia did. With Chingotto, Galán's partner. 6-3, 6-2 over Italy.

And what everyone expected since the draw happened: Spain against Argentina in both World Cup finals.

In Doha. Just like 2021. Just like 2024.

Spain: Goñi and Yanguas come back again

Spain beat Portugal 2-0.

  • Aimar Goñi and Mike Yanguas: 4-6, 6-3, 6-2 over the Deus brothers.
  • Juan Lebrón and Paquito Navarro: 6-2, 6-4 over Araújo and Graça.

Jon Sanz and Javi Leal, down for the third match, didn't get on court.

Goñi and Yanguas lost the first set. Just like on Monday against Sweden. And just like on Monday, they turned it around: an hour and 38 minutes on court.

Lebrón and Paquito closed out the tie in just over an hour.

Spain have played thirteen matches at the World Cup. They have won them all.

Argentina: Tapia and Chingotto, just like 2024

Argentina beat Italy 2-0.

  • Di Nenno and Augsburger: 6-1, 6-1 over Montiel and Abbate, in 49 minutes.
  • Tapia and Chingotto: 6-3, 6-2 over Patiniotis and Brambilla.

Alfonso and Libaak didn't play.

Tapia and Chingotto already played the final together two years ago. They won their match, against Lebrón and Galán.

Argentina, champions at the last two World Cups, have played fourteen matches and won fourteen.

The women: not a set dropped

Spain beat France 2-0.

  • Paula Josemaría and Gemma Triay: 6-3, 6-1 over Godallier and Mansart.
  • Claudia Fernández and Bea González: 6-0, 6-0 over Collombon and Bahurel, in 44 minutes.

Andrea Ustero and Martina Calvo didn't play.

Fourteen matches at the World Cup. Fourteen wins. Not a set dropped. Nineteen games conceded.

They are going for a seventh World Cup in a row. Carolina Navarro's first as head coach.

Argentina beat Italy 2-0. Osoro and Fassio, 6-2, 6-2. Brea and Jensen, 6-2, 6-3.

Doha, for the third time

Doha already hosted the World Cup in 2021 and 2024. Both times, both finals were Spain against Argentina.

  • 2021: Spain won both. In the men's final, Coello and Galán beat Sanyo and Tapia 6-1, 7-5.
  • 2024: one each. The women, 2-0 to Spain. The men, 2-1 to Argentina, with the last match decided in a third-set tie-break.

The finals, this Saturday

  • 12:00: the women. Spain v Argentina.
  • Afterwards: the men. Spain v Argentina.

Times are CET; in Argentina, four hours earlier. Where to watch, here.

Last Saturday, Coello and Tapia won Kuwait together. So did Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea.

This Saturday they play for the World Cup.

Each in their own shirt.

— Luis González
