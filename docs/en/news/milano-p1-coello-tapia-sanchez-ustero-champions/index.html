Coello and Tapia come back in Milan: three titles in three weeks

They dropped the first set to Augsburger and Lebrón and won 4-6, 6-3, 6-2. In the women's final, Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero beat Josemaría and González again: 7-6, 6-3.

By Luis González · 19 October 2026 · 1 min read

This time they were the ones coming back.

Augsburger and Lebrón, who turned around a 0-6 set against Galán and Chingotto on Saturday, took the first set of the final: 6-4.

Coello and Tapia didn't flinch. 6-3. Then 6-2. One hour and fifty-seven minutes.

Rotterdam, Düsseldorf and Milan. Three weeks, three titles.

Ari and Ustero, again

The women's final was a repeat of Rotterdam. And it ended the same way.

Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero beat Paula Josemaría and Bea González in straight sets: 7-6 (7-3 in the tie-break) and 6-3.

Three finals in a row for them, in Rotterdam, Düsseldorf and Milan. Two titles.

Final Winners Losers Score
Men Coello / Tapia Augsburger / Lebrón 4-6 · 6-3 · 6-2
Women Sánchez / Ustero Josemaría / González 7-6 · 6-3

— Luis González
