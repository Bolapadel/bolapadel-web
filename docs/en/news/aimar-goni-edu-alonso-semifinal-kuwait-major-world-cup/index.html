News · Major de Kuwait

Aimar Goñi will play his first Major semifinal before heading to the World Cup with Spain

With Edu Alonso, 6-1, 6-2 against Stupaczuk and Sanz, the fourth seeds. Today, in Kuwait, against Tello and Arce. Next week, Goñi plays the World Cup.

By Luis González · 30 October 2026 · 3 min read

Nine days ago I told you that Aimar Goñi was Spain's new face for the World Cup.

21 years old. From Pamplona. 1.90 m. World number 32.

Today he plays his first semifinal at a Major.

With Edu Alonso. In Kuwait. Three days before the World Cup in Doha begins.

6-1, 6-2

Across the net were Franco Stupaczuk and Jon Sanz, the fourth seeds.

It didn't even last an hour.

Goñi and Alonso won 71% of the points. They dropped serve only once. And in the second set they won 16 points in a row.

Sixteen.

Sanz, the hometown rival

Jon Sanz is also from Pamplona.

In November last year he played a FIP tournament in Mexico with Goñi.

Yesterday he was on the other side of the net. Next week they'll be on the same team: both are going to the World Cup with Spain.

So is Stupaczuk. With Argentina.

The first one

Goñi and Alonso have played together since April.

They already knew what a semifinal feels like: in May they reached one at the Asunción P2. Galán and Chingotto beat them.

At a Major, never. The quarterfinals were their ceiling. In Paris, in September, Galán and Chingotto stopped them there too.

Alonso, 25, had never gone past the quarterfinals at a Major either.

In Kuwait, Goñi and Alonso, the 11th seeds, have beaten Mariano González and Francisco Cabeza (7-5, 6-1), Javi Leal and Fran Guerrero, the seventh seeds (6-4, 6-4), and yesterday Stupaczuk and Sanz.

Next up, Tello and Arce

Juan Tello and Maxi Arce, the ninth seeds, beat Marc Sintes and Juan Zamora 6-4, 6-2. That ends the tournament for the youngest pair in the quarterfinals.

Alonso knows Tello well: they were partners until April. Together they reached the semifinals in Newgiza.

And Arce is also going to the World Cup. With Argentina.

Today, Goñi and Arce are rivals. Next week, each with his national team.

They play not before 14:00 Spanish time.

The other semifinal

Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia against Tino Libaak and Gonzalo Alfonso. Not before 18:00.

Libaak and Alfonso beat Martín Di Nenno and Paquito Navarro 7-6, 6-1. It's their first Major semifinal too.

Coello and Tapia beat Jairo Bautista and Lucas Bergamini 6-1, 6-4. In one hour.

The women

The top four seeds, all in the semifinals.

Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero needed almost three hours to come back against Tamara Icardo and Claudia Jensen: 3-6, 6-4, 7-5. Today, at midday, they face Bea González and Paula Josemaría, who beat Lorena Rufo and Victoria Iglesias 6-2, 6-4. It's the Rotterdam and Milan final again.

Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea (6-1, 6-0 against Giulia Dal Pozzo and Nuria Rodríguez) will play Claudia Fernández and Martina Calvo (6-1, 6-0 against Marta Ortega and Sofía Araújo).

Before Doha

Goñi has spent a week in Kuwait. Next week, Doha. With the national team.

First, a semifinal.

The first one.

— Luis González
