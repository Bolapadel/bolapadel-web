These are Argentina's 16 for the World Cup in Qatar

Eight men and eight women. The men are defending the title and going for a third in a row. The women want revenge after two finals lost to Spain.

By Luis González · 22 October 2026 · 3 min read

Eleven days.

From 2 to 7 November, Doha hosts the World Cup again. The same city where Argentina were crowned champions two years ago.

The Argentine Padel Association already has its players. Eight and eight.

The men: going for a third in a row

Gaby Reca and Rodri Ovide are in charge. This is the team, with their position in this week's FIP ranking:

Player Rank
Agustín Tapia 1
Federico Chingotto 3
Leandro Augsburger 6
Franco Stupaczuk 7
Martín Di Nenno 12
Gonzalo Alfonso 21
Maximiliano Arce 25
Valentino Libaak 30

Tapia shares number 1 with Arturo Coello. His partner. In Doha they'll be on opposite sides of the net.

Six of them were part of the 2024 champion team.

There are two new faces. Maxi Arce, from Salta, left-handed, plays on the forehand side. He replaces Sanyo Gutiérrez. Gonzalo Alfonso, from General Cabrera, plays on the backhand side. He replaces Álex Chozas. For both, it's their first World Cup with the senior team.

And then there's Libaak.

He has the fewest points of the eight. But the last World Cup was decided in his match. He and Augsburger won the deciding point against Yanguas and Paquito Navarro. 2-1 over Spain.

This week they launched their own brand together.

Argentina have twelve men's world titles. The last two in a row: Dubai 2022 and Doha 2024.

The women: out for revenge

This is their list, also with this week's FIP ranking:

Player Rank
Delfina Brea 1
Claudia Jensen 11
Aranzazu Osoro 17
Martina Fassio 23
Virginia Riera 39
Julieta Bidahorria 42
María Laura Ferreyra 88
Victoria Vílchez 357

Delfi Brea is world number 1. She shares it with Gemma Triay, her partner. Just like Tapia and Coello: in Doha, each with her own flag.

The new name is Victoria Vílchez. She's 16 and from Río Cuarto, in Córdoba.

A few weeks ago she won gold at the South American Games in Santa Fe, with María Belén Mosca. It was the first time padel awarded medals at those Games. Now she joins the national team and Daiara Valenzuela misses out.

Here the story is different.

Spain have won the last six women's World Cups. The last two in a final against Argentina.

Two finals. Two defeats. That thorn is still there.

Eleven days

You already know Spain's 16.

Now they're all set.

The men are defending. The women want revenge.

— Luis González
