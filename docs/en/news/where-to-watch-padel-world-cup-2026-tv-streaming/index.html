Where to watch the World Cup in Qatar on TV and streaming

Movistar Plus+ in Spain and Andorra. ESPN across the Americas. Red Bull TV, free, almost everywhere else. And the FIP's YouTube channel for whatever TV doesn't show.

By Luis González · 2 November 2026 · 2 min read

Doha · 2-7 nov Mundial 2026

The World Cup in Qatar starts today.

And the first question isn't who wins it. It's where to watch it.

Here it is. Country by country, according to the broadcast list published by the International Padel Federation.

In Spain

Movistar Plus+. Also in Andorra.

Spain play today. The men, at 16:00 (CET), against Sweden. The women open the day: at 12:00, against Germany.

Watch the court. Neither team plays on centre court today: the men are on Grand Stand 2; the women, on court 5.

Whatever a country's TV doesn't show can be followed on the FIP's YouTube channel, @PADELFIP, according to Padel Addict.

In Argentina and the rest of the Americas

ESPN. From South America to Central America and the Caribbean.

Argentina, going for a third World Cup in a row, start today against Australia, at 12:00 Argentina time. The women, at 8:00, against Chile.

In the rest of the world

Red Bull TV shows it all over the planet, for free. Except in countries with an exclusive local deal.

Where Red Bull TV doesn't reach, the FIP's YouTube channel.

And each country, its own channel:

  • Italy, San Marino and Vatican City: SuperTennis.
  • Portugal: Sport TV.
  • Netherlands: KNLTB.
  • Bulgaria: bTV.
  • Croatia: SPTV.
  • Greece: MagentaTV.
  • Israel: Charlton.
  • beIN Sports: France (with Monaco, Switzerland and the overseas territories), United States, Canada, Turkey, the Middle East, North Africa, Australia, New Zealand, Thailand, Malaysia, the Philippines, Indonesia and Singapore.

When

From Monday 2 to Saturday 7 November, in Doha.

Group stage until Wednesday. Quarter-finals, Thursday. Semi-finals, Friday. And on Saturday, the finals.

You already have the groups, Spain's 16 and Argentina's.

Now you know where to look.

— Luis González
