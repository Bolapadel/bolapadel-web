These are the groups for the World Cup in Qatar

Argentina, the champions, with France, Australia and the Netherlands. Spain, with the UAE, Sweden and Paraguay. The Spanish women, with Brazil, Germany and Great Britain. It starts tomorrow in Doha.

By Luis González · 1 November 2026 · 3 min read

On Saturday, Coello and Tapia won the Kuwait Major together. So did Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea.

From tomorrow, each of them under their own flag.

Today the groups for the World Cup in Qatar were drawn. 32 national teams: 16 in the men's draw and 16 in the women's. From 2 to 7 November, at the Khalifa International Tennis and Squash Complex in Doha.

The men

  • Group A: Argentina, France, Australia and the Netherlands.
  • Group B: Spain, United Arab Emirates, Sweden and Paraguay.
  • Group C: Portugal, Chile, Qatar and Germany.
  • Group D: Italy, Brazil, United States and Mexico.

Argentina are defending the title. They won the last two World Cups, Dubai 2022 and Doha 2024. They're going for a third in a row.

In their group is Australia, back at a World Cup eight years later. They earned their place this week in Kuwait, coming from behind against Egypt in the final of the Asia-Africa qualifiers.

Spain get the UAE, Sweden and Paraguay.

Two years ago they lost the final right here, in Doha. Against Argentina, 2-1. They return without Ale Galán, out injured. His place goes to Javi Leal.

Portugal, who took bronze in 2024, their first ever medal, share a group with the hosts, Qatar. Italy have Brazil, the United States and Mexico.

Argentina and Spain are in different groups. In the first stage, Coello and Tapia won't meet.

The women

  • Group A: Spain, Brazil, Germany and Great Britain.
  • Group B: Argentina, France, Chile and Belgium.
  • Group C: Italy, Sweden, Egypt and Indonesia.
  • Group D: Portugal, Netherlands, United States and Paraguay.

Spain have won the last six women's World Cups. They're going for a seventh in a row. And they arrive with Gemma Triay just crowned the player with the most titles in history.

Argentina, runners-up in the last two editions, have France, Chile and Belgium.

Egypt and Indonesia are the last to arrive. They earned their places this week, also in Kuwait.

How it works

Each team faces the other three in its group. Each tie, best of three matches.

The top two in each group go through to the quarter-finals. The rest keep playing all week for places 9 to 16.

  • Monday 2 to Wednesday 4: group stage.
  • Thursday 5: quarter-finals.
  • Friday 6: semi-finals.
  • Saturday 7: in the morning, the bronze-medal matches. Then the finals, on the centre court.

There's €1.2 million in prize money, split equally between men and women. No World Cup had ever paid out this much.

What time

The women start at 12:00 in Spain (CET). The men, at 16:00. In Doha it's 14:00 and 18:00. In Argentina, 8:00 and 12:00.

You already know Spain's 16 and Argentina's.

Until Saturday, no partners.

Only flags.

— Luis González
