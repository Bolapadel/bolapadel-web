Garrido and De Pascual beat Stupaczuk and Sanz to reach the Dubai P1 semi-finals

A year after his last semi-final, also in Dubai, Javi Garrido is back in one. With Juani De Pascual, who at 21 plays his first in Premier.

By Luis González · 13 November 2026 · 4 min read

Javi Garrido is back in a semi-final.

Today, with Juani De Pascual, he beat Stupaczuk and Sanz (3) 6-1, 7-5. In an hour and 16 minutes.

Some semi-finals are worth celebrating a little more. This is one of them.

A year later

His last Premier semi-final was right here, in Dubai. On 15 November 2025, with Lucas Campagnolo. They lost to Coello and Tapia: 6-4, 7-6.

Since then, a whole year of coming close.

In 2026 he had stopped five times in the quarter-finals. Riyadh, Gijón, Newgiza and Rome, with Lucas Bergamini. Milan, already with De Pascual.

At the sixth attempt, he's through.

A six-week partnership

Garrido and De Pascual started playing together in early October. Dubai is their fourth Premier tournament.

Juani De Pascual is 21. He's from Pergamino (Argentina), left-handed, and plays on the right. It's his first Premier Padel semi-final.

Garrido, 26, from Córdoba, plays on the left. He knows what this feels like: with Mike Yanguas he reached the final of the 2024 Qatar Major.

In Milan, in October, they were one step away. They lost the quarter-final to Lebrón and Augsburger: 7-6, 6-3.

The road

  • Round of 32: 6-3, 7-6 against Gil and Valenzuela.
  • Round of 16: 7-6, 3-6, 7-6 against Yanguas and Nieto (5). Two tie-breaks and two hours and 34 minutes. On the other side, Yanguas, his partner in that Qatar final.
  • Quarter-finals: 6-1, 7-5 against Stupaczuk and Sanz (3). Jon Sanz became world champion with Spain on Saturday.

On Saturday, Libaak and Alfonso

In the semi-finals, Tino Libaak and Gonzalo Alfonso (10). Today they came back against Bautista and Bergamini (11): 4-6, 6-3, 7-6.

Bergamini was Garrido's partner until September.

And with Bergamini, Garrido already faced Libaak and Alfonso this year. It was in the Madrid P1 round of 32, in September. They won: 6-3, 7-6.

Now, with a new partner, a later round and another chance.

The rest of the day

Lebrón and Augsburger (2) beat Arce and Tello (8): 6-3, 3-6, 6-2, in an hour and 46 minutes. It's the fourth time they've beaten them this year. We told you about the other three yesterday.

In the semi-finals they'll face Flavio Abbate and Franco Dal Bianco, who beat Momo and Campagnolo (7): 7-6, 3-6, 6-2, in two hours and 12 minutes. It's their first semi-final together.

With Coello and Tapia and Galán and Chingotto missing, Dubai will have new champions on Sunday.

Women

The surprise of the day came from Tamara Icardo and Claudia Jensen (6). They beat Triay and Brea, the top seeds: 6-3, 3-6, 6-1, in two hours and three minutes.

  • Josemaría and Bea González (2): 6-3, 6-1 against Guinart and Alonso (7).
  • Ari and Andrea (3): 6-2, 6-3 against Velasco and Eugenio.
  • Calvo and Claudia Fernández (4): 7-6, 6-4 against Rufo and Iglesias.

Saturday's semi-finals

Spanish time (CET), approximate.

Women:

  • 13:00 · Jensen / Icardo (6) – Calvo / Fernández (4)
  • 15:00 · Ari / Andrea (3) – Josemaría / Bea González (2)

Men:

  • 17:00 · Abbate / Dal Bianco – Lebrón / Augsburger (2)
  • 18:30 · Libaak / Alfonso (10) – De Pascual / Garrido (14)

A year ago, Garrido left Dubai in the semi-finals.

This Saturday he's there again. And this time, the door is a little more open.

— Luis González
