Coello and Tapia complete the double: Rotterdam and Düsseldorf

Two P2s in two weeks. Two finals against Stupaczuk and Sanz. Two titles.

By Luis González · 12 October 2026 · 1 min read

Same opponents. Same ending.

In Rotterdam, on 4 October: 6-3, 6-2 against Franco Stupaczuk and Jon Sanz. Sixty-eight minutes.

Seven days later, in Düsseldorf, the same pair across the net. This one took a bit more work: 6-2, 7-6, with the second-set tie-break closed out 7-5.
