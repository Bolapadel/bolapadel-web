News · Major de Kuwait

Coello and Tapia win the Kuwait Major and will finish 2026 as number one

6-1, 6-1 over Arce and Tello in 55 minutes. With the title, no one can catch them in the Race. It's their fourth straight year on top. In the women's final, Triay and Brea came from behind against Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero.

By Luis González · 31 October 2026

News · 31 Oct

Arce and Tello will face Coello and Tapia in the Kuwait final; Ari and Andrea take on Triay and Brea

A week ago, Arce and Tello lost a final in Melilla. Today they play a Major final. Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero come through a semifinal of more than three and a half hours. Against Triay and Brea it's six wins each this year.

News · 30 Oct

Javi Leal replaces Galán at the World Cup in Qatar

The Spanish Padel Federation announced it on Friday, hours after Ale Galán pulled out. Javi Leal, 23 and world number 16, will play his first World Cup.

News · 30 Oct

Ale Galán will miss the World Cup in Qatar through injury

The Spanish Padel Federation announced it on Friday. After missing the Kuwait Major, Galán won't be in Doha either. The World Cup starts on Monday.

News · 30 Oct

Aimar Goñi will play his first Major semifinal before heading to the World Cup with Spain

With Edu Alonso, 6-1, 6-2 against Stupaczuk and Sanz, the fourth seeds. Today, in Kuwait, against Tello and Arce. Next week, Goñi plays the World Cup.

News · 29 Oct

Libaak and Alfonso beat Lebrón and Augsburger again and will face Di Nenno and Navarro

They had already beaten them in Madrid. Yesterday, in Kuwait, they did it again: in the third set, Lebrón and Augsburger won just one game. Sintes and Zamora are also into the quarterfinals. Yanguas and Nieto are out.

News · 28 Oct

Lebrón and Augsburger beat Abud and Belluati and will face Libaak and Alfonso

The qualifiers' run is over. Coello and Tapia, in under an hour, and Yanguas and Nieto, in three sets, are also into the round of 16. Today, Augsburger faces his national team partner.
