Spain, world champions, give all the credit to Juanjo Gutiérrez

Coello, Lebrón and Paquito said it in the Doha press room: the head coach's decisions shaped the final. Paquito, who is saying goodbye to the national team, started by thanking him.

News · 7 Nov

Spain beat Argentina in both World Cup finals in Qatar

The men won 2-1 and ended Argentina's run as 2022 and 2024 champions. Coello and Nieto sealed the final. The women won 2-0: a seventh straight World Cup.

News · 7 Nov

Spain win the women's World Cup in Qatar for a seventh title in a row

Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero beat Osoro and Fassio 6-3, 6-3. Paula Josemaría and Gemma Triay sealed the 2-0 against Brea and Jensen: 6-2, 6-7, 6-3. A tenth World Cup for Spain.

News · 7 Nov

Italy beat France for a fourth straight bronze at the women's World Cup

Italy's women beat France 2-0 in the third-place match. Bronze in 2021, in 2022, in 2024 and now again in Doha.

News · 6 Nov

Spain and Argentina will meet in both World Cup finals in Qatar

Spain beat Portugal 2-0 and the women beat France 2-0. Argentina beat Italy 2-0 in both draws. On Saturday, a double final in Doha, just like 2021 and 2024.

News · 5 Nov

Spain and Argentina put both their teams into the World Cup semi-finals in Qatar

Spain beat Brazil 2-0, with Coello and Javi Leal on the same side, and Argentina beat Germany 3-0. The women, two more 3-0s. They can only meet in Saturday's finals.
